Privacy Policy for GerrymanderX

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

1. Introduction

GerrymanderX ("the App") is a precinct-level election atlas for macOS developed by com.swordfish. It lets you download public U.S. election result databases, explore them on a map, compare elections, and draw your own groupings of precincts. This policy explains what data the App handles and — more importantly — what it does not. The short version: the App asks you to sign in so it knows who you are, and nothing else about you is collected; everything you download or create stays on your Mac.

2. Account and Sign-In

The App requires an account. You create one by signing in with Google — there is no separate registration form and no password held by us. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google LLC, acting as our processor.

When you sign in, we receive from Google and store the following about you:

  • Your name and profile picture, as they appear on your Google account. These are shown to you in the App's side menu and nowhere else.
  • Your email address, which identifies the account.
  • A user identifier generated by Firebase, plus the dates your account was created and last used.

That is the entire account record. We use it only to let you into the App and to show you which account you are signed in as. It is not used for advertising or profiling, it is never sold or shared, and we do not receive your Google password or any other content of your Google account.

Your signed-in session is kept in the macOS Keychain on your Mac so you do not have to sign in every time. Signing out removes it.

3. Data We Collect

Beyond the account record described in Section 2, nothing. The App collects no usage analytics, no crash reports, no location data and no advertising identifiers, and it contains no advertising or third-party tracking SDKs. Nothing you do inside the App — which elections you open, which regions you inspect, which layers you draw — is recorded or transmitted to us.

4. Network Activity

The App makes network requests in exactly two situations:

  • Signing in — an authorization exchange with Google's sign-in service and Firebase Authentication, which happens when you press the sign-in button and, briefly, when your session is renewed.
  • Downloading public election data from a content delivery server operated on our behalf: the election list, a small manifest of what is available, and election databases, one per election and state, downloaded only when you explicitly click Download or Download All. Interrupted downloads may be resumed automatically the next time you open the Remote list.

The data downloads are plain file requests. They carry no account, identifier or user-generated data; the hosting provider sees only the standard network metadata any download discloses (such as your IP address and the requested file). Apart from signing in, nothing is ever uploaded from the App.

5. What Is Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following, entirely on your Mac, inside ~/Documents/GerrymanderX/:

  • Downloaded election databases and their accompanying metadata (candidate and party lists). These contain public election results and boundary geometry only.
  • Your custom layers — the layer names, group names, colours and the sets of precincts you assign to each group, kept in a local SQLite database. These are your own creations and exist nowhere else.
  • Preferences — currently your theme choice (System, Light or Dark).

None of this is linked to your account. Your custom layers and downloads belong to the Mac they were made on: they are not uploaded, not synchronised between devices, and not attached to your identity. Signing in on a different Mac will not bring them with you, and signing in as a different person on the same Mac shows the same locally stored data.

You can delete any downloaded election or custom layer from within the App; deleting an election also deletes the custom layers that depend on it. Removing the ~/Documents/GerrymanderX/ folder removes everything the App has stored on your device.

6. Data Retention and Deleting Your Account

Your account record is kept until you delete it. You can delete it at any time from within the App, under Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your name, email address, profile picture and user identifier from Firebase Authentication; it cannot be undone. You may also write to us at the address in Section 11 and we will delete it for you.

Deleting your account does not touch the data on your Mac, because that data was never connected to the account. Remove it separately as described in Section 5.

7. Data Sources

The election data the App offers for download is compiled from public sources: precinct results and boundaries published by the Voting and Election Science Team (VEST) via the Redistricting Data Hub and by the MIT Election Data + Science Lab (MEDSL), and state, county and congressional-district boundaries from the U.S. Census Bureau. This data describes aggregate election results by geographic area and contains no information about individual voters.

8. Links You Open

The App may open this policy in your browser, and the Feedback action opens your email client with our support address and the App version pre-filled — nothing is sent until you send it yourself. Those sites and apps are governed by their own policies.

9. Third-Party Components

Signing in relies on Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In, both provided by Google LLC; their handling of the data described in Section 2 is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase Privacy and Security information. Apart from these, the App is built with open-source software (Flutter and SQLite, among others), which runs locally and transmits nothing.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children. Signing in requires a Google account, which is subject to Google's own minimum age requirements. We collect no data from children beyond the account record any signed-in user provides.

11. Changes to This Policy

If the App's behavior ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated and the "Last Updated" date revised before the change ships.
